## Position and boundary

The site should help couples, families, planners, designers, and procurement teams choose, prepare, proof, order, receive, and distribute dimensional pop-up invitations. It owns mechanism/model selection, paper/print/finish, artwork/data preparation, prototypes, customization, production planning, packaging, readability, QA, and procurement.

It does not own tents or temporary structures, full wedding planning, venue management, generic invitation-by-city pages, copyrighted character copying, or unsupported price/production claims.

## Evidence gates

- Paper weight/caliper, fold life, dimensional limits, colour, coating, adhesion, foil, die-cut tolerance, durability, recyclability, and safety claims require named material/process data and approved physical samples.
- Prices, minimum quantities, waste, production time, delivery, reprint, payment, and warranty terms require dated written supplier evidence.
- Colour and mechanism approval require a representative physical prototype; screen images alone are insufficient.
- Names, addresses, contact details, guest lists, and RSVP data require consent, access control, correction workflow, and deletion/retention decisions.
- Fonts, illustrations, characters, photographs, maps, trademarks, and cultural motifs require appropriate rights and respectful use.
